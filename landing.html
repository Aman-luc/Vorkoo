The Challenge

Handling multiple jobs shouldn't feel like mental gymnastics

When work items are spread across notebooks, emails, and spreadsheet rows, essential details slip through the cracks.

Scattered Priorities

Without a single source of truth, it is hard to quickly know which deliverable needs your immediate focus today.

Approaching Deadlines

Deliverables get forgotten or delayed when milestone dates live across calendar apps and buried email threads.

Unclear Payment Status

Uncertainty around total project value, partial deposits, and remaining balances makes financial tracking tedious.

The Vorkoo System

Work is the center of everything.
Everything else connects to it.

Vorkoo replaces scattered notes and disconnected tools with one organized workspace. Every client, deadline, and payment belongs directly to the work item it belongs to.

  • ✓
    Work Comes First: Create an entry for every project, service, or task you are delivering.
  • ✓
    Clients & Deadlines: Assign clients, set urgency levels, and keep target dates clear.
  • ✓
    Clear Payment Tracking: Record agreed fees and payments received so you always know what is still due.

Core Features

Everything you need for work management

Purpose-built tools designed to give you calm control over daily operations.

Work Management

Create, edit, and maintain structured records for every project. Maintain complete descriptions, clients, priorities, and deadlines in one place.

Priority & Status Control

Categorize work into Low, Medium, or High priority levels. Track lifecycle stages easily with Pending, In Progress, and Completed states.

Client Management

Organize contact and business information for all your clients. Connect multiple active or past works to a single client record easily.

Payment Tracking

Log agreed work amounts, manual payments received, and view outstanding balances without leaving your work view.

Central Dashboard

Get immediate visual feedback on key operational metrics: total work counts, status distributions, upcoming deadlines, total received funds, and pending balances.
